# Insta Reels – Oct 12 2026 – edited

Source: Drive folder "Insta Reels - Oct 12 2026" (9 A-rolls, 9 reference reels, 1 B-roll).

## Look (same as the Oct 11 edits)
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, black canvas; video cropped to 4:3 (1080x810) at y=564.
- Title: Liberation Sans 53 px, name in bold, white, left-aligned at x=94, sitting just above the video.
  Every title is 1.5 lines: line 2 is 45–58% of the width of line 1.
- Captions: one word at a time, Title Case, DejaVu Serif Bold ~84 px, white with a dark outline and soft drop shadow,
  0.1 s crossfade between words, hidden during pauses. Commas and question marks kept, full stops dropped.
  Swear words are starred out the same way the reference reels do it.
- Colour grade: gentle S-curve, warm highlights / cool shadows, +16% saturation, light sharpening, soft vignette
  (no vignette on the Naval reel because of its white background).
- Audio: loudness-normalised to -14 LUFS, short fades on every jump cut.

## How each edit follows its reference
The cuts were taken from the reference reels by matching their audio against the A-roll, so every jump cut the
reference makes (removed pauses, dropped filler) is in the edit too. Word timings come from forced alignment of
the reference's caption text against the A-roll audio.

- 01 – Opens on the Steve Jobs B-roll while Kevin says "Steve, by the way. Not a nice guy." (the reference opens on
  the same B-roll). Ends on "Look how wildly successful he was." The A-roll file stops at 61 s, partway through
  the next point (the signal-to-noise idea), so that part of the reference can't be built.
- 02 – The A-roll file is only 17.9 s; it ends at "...I may charge you 150K". The reference runs 69 s.
- 03 – No B-roll for the airport opening in the reference, so it starts straight on 50 Cent.
- 04 – Cropped to James's camera box inside the Fox Business split-screen (no ticker or other panel in frame).
- 05 – The reference covers this with film B-roll and kinetic text; only the A-roll was provided, so it plays on Elon.
  A-roll ends at 18 s ("There's gotta be some things like that").
- 06 – Full reference cut, all jump cuts matched. From "External validation becomes…" to "…a complete game" the A-roll
  has its own burned-in "CHAMATH'S PLAYBOOK" text in the top-left. The reference left it half in frame; this edit
  punches in to a tighter crop at the jump cuts so it's out of shot, then goes back to the wide shot for the last line.
- 07 – The reference's opening ("I'm 39… you became a millionaire at 17") is not in the A-roll file, so the edit starts
  at "If you had to start from zero". Ends on "...and grow that business" (A-roll ends 1 s later, mid-sentence).
  Cropped above the A-roll's burned-in yellow subtitles.
- 08 – A-roll file is only 10.4 s (one complete thought). Cropped inside the vertical video, above its burned-in subtitles
  and the "Limitless Motivation" watermark.
- 09 – Ends on "...it will lose all its value." (the A-roll stops mid-sentence after that). The caption outline is
  thicker here so the white words stay readable on the white background.
